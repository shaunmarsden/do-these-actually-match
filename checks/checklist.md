# Human Review Checklist

Go through this before you act on anything it finds.

- [ ] Every row reported as a real disagreement has had differences in presentation and format ruled out first
- [ ] No row was forced to match another just to make the totals line up, and anything unclear is marked "could not confidently match" rather than guessed either way
- [ ] Every row that's in only one record is reported in its own group, not folded into a mismatch
- [ ] Nothing says which source is right, only that they disagree and by how much
- [ ] No reason was made up for a disagreement beyond what the two records show
- [ ] If the two records were taken at noticeably different times, the gap is stated, not ignored
