# Human Review Checklist

Run through this before acting on any finding.

- [ ] Every row reported as a genuine disagreement has actually had presentation and format differences ruled out first
- [ ] No row was force-matched to another just to make the totals line up, and anything genuinely ambiguous is marked "could not confidently match" rather than guessed either way
- [ ] Every row present in only one record is reported as its own category, not folded into a mismatch
- [ ] Nothing states which source is correct, only that they disagree and by how much
- [ ] No reason was invented for a genuine disagreement beyond what the two records actually show
- [ ] If the two records were captured at meaningfully different times, that gap is stated, not silently ignored
